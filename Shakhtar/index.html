About the Club

"Shakhtar Donetsk" is a Ukrainian football club based in the city of Donetsk, founded in 1936. The club is one of the most successful in Ukrainian football history and regularly participates in national and international tournaments.

Shakhtar has won numerous Ukrainian championships and cups and achieved significant success in European competitions, including winning the UEFA Cup in 2009.

Achievements

  • 13-time Ukrainian Premier League Champions
  • 13-time Ukrainian Cup Winners
  • 8-time Ukrainian Super Cup Winners
  • UEFA Cup Winner 2008-09

Stadium

Shakhtar's home stadium is the "Donbass Arena" in Donetsk. This modern stadium holds over 52,000 spectators and is one of the finest stadiums in Eastern Europe.
